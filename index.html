Our Story

Built by
Enthusiasts.
For Everyone.

D_MANDEM was born out of a simple frustration: finding authentic mechanical keyboards and components in the Philippines was either overpriced, sketchy, or both. We're a team of keyboard addicts based in Pampanga who decided to fix that.

Every board, switch, and keycap set we carry has been personally tested by our team. We don't list anything we wouldn't use ourselves. From budget friendly entry boards to high-end enthusiast builds we've got your hands covered.
